The Levy Spring Convening brings together founders, senior public servants, and institutional leaders for a day of structured conversation on the operating conditions that matter most.

This is not a conference. There are no panels, no keynote speakers, no name badges. It is a working session designed around real problems, with people who can contribute at the level the problems require.

## Format

The convening is organized around three working sessions, each addressing a specific structural challenge identified by network members. Sessions are facilitated but not scripted. The goal is collective intelligence, not performance.

## Who should attend

Network members at the Builder and Architect tiers have priority access. A limited number of seats are available for invited guests and Insider-tier members on a case-by-case basis.

## Details

- **Date:** May 15, 2026
- **Location:** Washington, D.C. (venue confirmed upon registration)
- **Duration:** Full day (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM)
- **Dinner:** An informal dinner follows for attendees who wish to continue the conversation.
